# CluoGames - Daily Puzzle Collection

## App Store Description

**Challenge your mind with three addictive daily puzzles in one beautiful app!**

CluoGames brings together three classic puzzle games that will keep your brain sharp and entertained. Each day brings fresh challenges designed to test your logic, word skills, and mathematical thinking.

### 🧩 **Three Daily Puzzles:**

**Sudoku** - The classic number puzzle that never gets old. Fill each row, column, and 3x3 box with digits 1-9 without repeats. Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulties.

**Unscramble** - Word puzzle meets Wordle! Unscramble the scrambled word in 6 tries. Use color-coded feedback to guide your guesses and solve today's word challenge.

**Exacto** - A unique math puzzle where you use six numbers and multiplication to hit the target. Think strategically to find the perfect combination.
